# Original artwork prompts

All four assets were generated with the built-in Codex imagegen tool in this
session, before or during interface development. No API key or external image
service was used. Originals remain unmodified in this directory. The icon's app
rendition was resized to 1024×1024 for the asset catalog. No generated screenshot
is presented as evidence that the app runs.

## 1. Visual target — visual-target-v1.png

Use case: ui-mockup. Create the definitive visual target art direction board for
BRACKISH, an exceptional native iPhone NYC fishing field journal. Landscape design
board showing three large perfectly flat iPhone screen compositions side by side,
no device mockup perspective. Screen 1: cinematic dark jade underwater New York
harbor, physically plausible amber shafts of surface light, silvery striped bass
lower half, handwritten small observational marks; top small BRACKISH / NEW YORK
CITY, elegant huge cream editorial serif title 'A different kind of city.'; bottom
restrained cream pill 'Find your water'. Screen 2: warm ivory personal field journal
with deep ink serif 'The field journal', exquisite small pencil and watercolor fish
study, fine editorial ruled dividers, generous margins, a saved catch entry with
real photographic crop, rust orange accent, small rounded bottom tab bar. Screen 3:
dark ink teal fishing atlas map with NYC shoreline, restrained numbered orange pins,
cream bottom location sheet 'Gantry Plaza' with small editorial data and clear native
controls. Original mascot Pip: small thoughtful illustrated harbor fish, muted sea
green and warm rust fins, subtle dot eyes, tiny field notebook beside him,
sophisticated natural-history watercolor and pencil rather than cartoon toy; display
a separate Pip sketch with color palette swatches below phones. Art direction:
cinematic underwater atmosphere plus personal naturalist journal. Beautiful restrained
typography, large left aligned New York serif headlines, understated sans secondary,
no generic dashboard cards, no emoji, no gratuitous glass or neon. Target precise
authored high-end indie design studio polish. Use exact requested visible text,
no logos from existing products. This is a design reference, not an actual app screenshot.

## 2. Harbor — harbor-original.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-bleed portrait background artwork for
Brackish native iPhone NYC fishing journal onboarding. Produce a tall portrait
1024x1536 original cinematic underwater harbor scene. Deep dark jade teal water,
surface ripples high overhead, physically believable angled ivory sunlight filtering
from top center, delicate suspended particles, rich moody dark corners and shadow.
A single magnificent silver striped bass swimming leftward across the lower middle,
weathered timber piling at far right fading into dark, tiny estuary vegetation at
bottom. Premium natural-history realism with very subtle film grain, quiet and
contemplative, not tropical, no coral. Composition: upper 55 percent mostly clean
dark teal water with gentle light for large cream interface headline to be added
later. Fish fully within width with breathing room at sides. Bottom 15 percent deep
shadow for a button overlay. No words, no letters, no UI, no frame, no watermark.
Palette deep #08282A, #174744, silvery warm pearl, tiny amber highlights. Beautiful
indie studio art direction, actual plausible aquatic light and material response.

## 4. Icon — icon-original.png

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: iOS app icon, square 1024x1024 with no transparency
and no corner rounding. Create a sophisticated original icon for Brackish, an NYC
fishing field journal. A single elegant silver-ivory striped bass silhouette swims
left in a deep near-black jade green square. The fish is centered in the middle 65
percent, drawn as a beautiful restrained natural-history woodcut with hand-engraved
dark parallel stripes and a small rust-orange eye. A thin ivory circular water-ripple
line behind the fish quietly evokes a field observation. Flat ink aesthetic with very
subtle paper grain, simple unmistakable silhouette at tiny sizes, lots of dark breathing
room. No text, no letters, no UI, no mockup, no gradients, no glow, no extra fish.
Warm paper #F2EAD9, ink #092426, tiny rust #A64F2C. Exceptional authored editorial branding.
